# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

CopyDesk is a place for clear, respectful work. Contributors must focus on the issue, the code, or the documentation rather than on another person.

We do not accept harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, or deliberate disruption.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming language.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept correction without hostility.
- Respect different views and experience.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Reporting

Report a conduct concern to the maintainer through the [issue tracker](https://github.com/carlosboeing/copydesk/issues) without private details. Use [GitHub's private advisory form](https://github.com/carlosboeing/copydesk/security/advisories/new) for sensitive safety or security concerns.

The maintainer will review reports and decide what action is appropriate. A report may lead to a warning, removal from a discussion, or a ban from the project.
